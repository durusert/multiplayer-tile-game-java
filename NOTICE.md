# Portfolio publication notice

This repository is a sanitized source-code portfolio derived from a collaborative university software-engineering project.

## Authorship

The original project began with six contributors and was completed by a five-person team. The clean history in this public repository is not a replacement for the original private GitLab history and must not be interpreted as sole authorship by the portfolio owner.

## Excluded material

The following material from the private academic submission is intentionally excluded:

- Commercial board-game artwork, tile sheets, boards, and scoring graphics
- Music and sound effects without repository-ready redistribution records
- Compiled platform-specific JAR files
- Generated Javadoc output
- Internal university/course documents and team process notes
- Local preview styles containing developer filesystem paths
- Large wireframe exports and other nonessential binaries
